COMPRESSR - Video in. DVD out.

Extract the whole ZIP first, then double-click Compressr.exe.
Keep the entire folder together, including tools, DLLs and licenses.
No installer, .NET, SDK, PowerShell command or first-launch download is needed.
This bundle is for personal use on Windows 10/11 x64, not a signed public release.

PREPARE NOW
Drop your video files onto the app or add permitted YouTube links.
Arrange videos with Move up / Move down. Whole videos play in that order.
Choose PAL for UK players, and SingleLayer for a typical 4.7 GB DVD.
Click Prepare DVD / save ISO and choose a writable location.
No blank disc is required. Keep the saved ISO; it is ready to burn later.

BURN LATER
Open Compressr.exe, insert a compatible BLANK DVD and select the writer.
Click Burn prepared DVD, select your saved ISO and confirm image and drive.
No original videos are required. Compressr never erases a used disc.
Do not close the app or disconnect the drive during burning/read-back.
DVD+RW support varies between standalone players; test on your own player.

Use only media you own or have permission to download/copy.
YouTube needs internet; local conversion and burning do not.
YouTube playback timestamps are ignored; there is no trimming.
Allow several GB of free space, roughly three times disc capacity plus sources.
Settings/downloads/jobs are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Compressr, not this folder.
The folder is movable, but settings and temporary work are per Windows user.
If COMPRESSR_TOOLS is set, it overrides bundled tools; unset it for normal use.
